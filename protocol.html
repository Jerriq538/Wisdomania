The Wisdomania Protocol

WHAT IS
YOUR POSITION
IN LIFE?

Before a man can become sovereign, he must understand life.

Not the life he imagines.

The life he actually lives.

Then he must understand his position within it.

01 — POSITION

KNOW YOUR POSITION.

What does he have?

What does he lack?

What can he do?

What can he not do?

What is he responsible for?

What does his position require of him?

Because you cannot deliberately change a position you have never properly identified.

02 — REALITY

LIVE ACCORDING
TO YOUR POSITION.

Once you understand your position, you have to live according to it.

Not according to ego.

Not according to comparison.

Not according to the person you wish you were.

According to reality.

If you lack financial capability, develop it.

If you lack physical capability, develop it.

If you lack the capacity to lead yourself, develop it.

Your position tells you what must be developed.

03 — CAPABILITY

THREE DIMENSIONS
OF CAPABILITY.

A man may possess money and still lack strength. He may possess strength and still depend on others financially. He may command attention and still lack the capability to lead himself.

None of these are complete positions of sovereignty.

Sovereignty requires capability across the dimensions that determine your ability to govern your life.

FINANCE

Mastery of money, resources, value creation and ownership.

STRENGTH

Physical capability, resilience, discipline and health.

LEADERSHIP

The capacity to govern yourself, carry responsibility and direct action.

Three dimensions. One pursuit.
The capacity to govern your own life.

04 — ELEVATION

ELEVATE
YOUR POSITION.

Once you understand your position and live according to it, you can identify what must change.

What knowledge do you need?

What skills?

What habits?

What resources?

What actions?

What sacrifices?

Then you act.

You develop capability.

You elevate your position.

And when your position changes, what was required of you changes with it.

So you must learn to live according to the new position.

05 — REASSESS

REASSESS.

This is where most people misunderstand progress. Reaching a higher position does not mean the process is finished. It gives you a new position from which to begin again.

UNDERSTAND LIFE → KNOW YOUR POSITION → LIVE ACCORDING TO IT → IDENTIFY WHAT MUST CHANGE → ELEVATE YOUR POSITION → LIVE ACCORDING TO THE NEW POSITION → REASSESS → REPEAT
06 — MASTERY IN MOTION

THIS IS
MASTERY IN MOTION.

Not pretending you have arrived.

Not becoming wealthy while remaining incapable.

Not becoming strong while remaining dependent.

Not calling yourself a leader without developing the capacity to lead.

Know your position.

Accept its reality.

Develop what it requires.

Elevate it.

Then do it again.

Because sovereignty is not something you claim.

It is something you become capable of occupying.
